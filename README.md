# Formalizations of papers

Lean 4 / Mathlib formalizations of results from papers by Filip Najman. Each directory is a self-contained Lean
project, with its own toolchain, a [Comparator](https://github.com/leanprover/comparator) challenge stating the
formalized results over Mathlib alone, Palomar metadata (`formalization.yaml`), and a README.

| directory | paper | results |
|---|---|---|
| [`gaussian-quartics`](gaussian-quartics) | F. Najman, *The Diophantine equation x⁴ ± y⁴ = iz² in Gaussian integers*, Amer. Math. Monthly 117 (2010), 637–641, [arXiv:1005.1528](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.1528) | Theorems 1 and 2, the closing remark over ℚ(i), and the ℚ(i)-points of y² = x³ ± x: 9 theorems and 2 definitions, no `sorry` |
| [`najman-trbovic`](najman-trbovic) | F. Najman, A. Trbović, *Splitting of primes in number fields generated by points on some modular curves*, Res. Number Theory 8 (2022), Article 28, [arXiv:2009.02485](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.02485) | partial: Theorem 2.2 (Table 2), the square-residue part of Theorem 2.8, the arithmetic of Theorem 2.12, corrected Table 7 for the explicit models, and parts of Section 3, some under named hypotheses; no `sorry` |
| [`three-factorials`](three-factorials) | A. Dujella, F. Najman, N. Saradha, T. N. Shorey, *Products of three factorials*, Publ. Math. Debrecen 85 (2014), 123–130, [doi:10.5486/PMD.2014.5835](https://doi.org/10.5486/PMD.2014.5835) | partial: Theorem 1.1 at a₃ = 26 (a₁ = 324 and 2738 are the only solutions), the seven solutions of Theorem 1.1, Theorem 1.2(a), part (b) under a corrected hypothesis, part (c) for a₃ ≥ 101, and conditional versions of the rest; no `sorry` |

The formalization of the paper on normalizers and modular automorphisms is in a repository of its own,
[F-Najman/normalizer-lean](https://github.com/F-Najman/normalizer-lean).
